Reconstruction: GT vs PGSR vs MatSpray vs LTX

For each sample scene: the camera path over the top view of the PGSR reconstruction, and the same view as the Blender ground truth, the PGSR render, the MatSpray PBR re-render and the LTX + image refinement, all under the scene's capture light. LTX refines the MatSpray light-rotation clips, so its still (frame 0 of the capture-light clip) exists for the 3 relighting views only. Click a number on the map or a thumbnail to change the view; press 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 to flip between GT, PGSR, MatSpray and LTX. All images are the 768×768 center crop LTX works on, cut out with the GT mask (sky on grey); PSNR is measured inside that mask.
